      Official starting points

      Use current rules and licensed-entity registers, then add product- and class-specific evidence.

      • CBUAE Rulebook · Article 2: Classification of the Insurance Business — regulatory taxonomy; not proof of market demand or performance.
      • CBUAE · Licensing and registers — verify current status and permitted activity for each entity.
      • DFSA · Insurance supervision in DIFC — relevant to DIFC-regulated activity; do not merge jurisdictions.
      • ADGM FSRA · Financial services regulation — check the relevant rulebook and regulated-entity register.
      Evidence discipline: a legal classification confirms a recognised insurance line. It does not by itself establish market size, product availability, loss experience, customer demand, profitability or a commercial opportunity.
