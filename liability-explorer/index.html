Liability Insurance

Role & responsibilities

    Key relationships

    Map discipline: radial links describe ecosystem roles and interfaces, not proof that every party participates in every policy, claim or commercial placement. Specific relationships require evidence naming the parties and transaction.

    Public / Commercial General Liability (CGL)

    Third-party bodily injury or property damage arising from insured business operations, premises or specified activities. Coverage depends on wording, territorial/jurisdictional scope, limits and exclusions.

    • Premises and operational liability
    • Visitors, customers and third parties
    • Products/completed operations only where included
    • Sudden and accidental pollution may be a separate extension

    Product Liability

    Legal liability for bodily injury or property damage caused by a product manufactured, sold or supplied by the insured, subject to trigger, territory, retroactive/occurrence terms and exclusions.

    • Manufacturers, importers and distributors
    • Product recall is not the same as product liability
    • Recall costs often require separate cover

    Professional Indemnity (PI / E&O)

    Claims alleging negligent acts, errors or omissions in professional services. Many PI wordings are claims-made and notified, making retroactive dates and continuity critical.

    • Consultants, engineers, architects and accountants
    • Legal, financial and technology professionals
    • Defence costs and consent provisions

    Medical Malpractice

    Professional liability of healthcare professionals and institutions for alleged errors or omissions in clinical services. Distinct from health insurance and employee medical benefits.

    • Hospitals, clinics and diagnostic centres
    • Doctors, nurses and allied health professionals
    • Consent, scope of practice and clinical records

    Directors & Officers (D&O)

    Specified claims against directors and officers arising from alleged wrongful acts in managerial capacity. Common structures include Side A, Side B and Side C, subject to wording.

    • Governance and management decisions
    • Entity securities claims where insured
    • Investigation and defence costs

    Employers’ Liability

    Employer's legal liability for employee injury or disease, often alongside statutory/work injury arrangements. It is not interchangeable with workers’ compensation.

    • Employer negligence allegations
    • Common-law or other applicable liability
    • Check local law, policy and employee category

    Event, Media & Cyber Liability

    Specialist liability covers for event organisers, media activity and cyber incidents. Each responds to a defined exposure and may sit outside a standard CGL policy.

    • Event organiser liability
    • Media liability, defamation and content
    • Cyber privacy/network liability

    Construction & Decennial / Inherent Defects

    Construction-related liability, professional design exposures and statutory/contractual building defect arrangements. Decennial or inherent-defects structures are distinct from ordinary CGL.

    • Contractors and project owners
    • Design and construction professionals
    • Project-specific and statutory requirements

    Liability claims lifecycle

    Illustrative workflow; actual duties depend on policy wording, applicable law and claim circumstances.

    01

    Risk & proposal

    Business activities, revenue/payroll, services, products, locations, contracts, controls, claims history and requested limits.

    02

    Underwriting & placement

    Exposure analysis, policy form, limits, retentions, retroactive date, territorial scope, exclusions and reinsurance capacity.

    03

    Incident & notice

    Incident chronology, claimant details, immediate mitigation, preservation of documents and prompt insurer/broker notification.

    04

    Coverage assessment

    Insured status, trigger, policy period, alleged wrongful act, causation, liability, exclusions, conditions and applicable law.

    05

    Defence & adjustment

    Legal representation, experts, evidence, quantum, contribution, settlement authority and defence-cost treatment.

    06

    Settlement & recovery

    Settlement/judgment, payment, subrogation or contribution where available, reinsurance notification and recovery.

    A claim allegation is not proof of liability or coverage. Separate the legal liability question from the policy response question. Human legal, claims and underwriting review remains essential.

    Brokers & intermediaries

    • Gargash Insurance Services — UAE intermediary listing public, CGL, product, employers’, D&O, PI and medical malpractice liability solutions. Broker product catalogue
    • ASIB — liability broking/arranging pages spanning public liability, PI, products, employers’, D&O. Broker page
    • Specialist brokers may arrange complex, project, professional and multinational liability placements; confirm entity permissions and named placements separately.

    A broker's product catalogue or market access statement is not proof of an appointment or a placement with a named insurer.

    Regulatory & legal context

    • CBUAE — classifies insurance business, including PI, work accidents/employer liability and related liabilities within property/liability business. Rulebook Article 2
    • UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 48 of 2023 — regulates insurance activities; compulsory insurance may be imposed by relevant resolutions. Legislation
    • UAE Civil Transactions Law — civil liability framework; use current law text and legal advice for claim-specific interpretation. UAE Legislation portal

    Do not assume every liability product is compulsory across all businesses. Mandatory requirements are activity-, authority-, contract- and jurisdiction-specific.
