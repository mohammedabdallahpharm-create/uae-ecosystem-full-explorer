Role & responsibilities

    Key relationships

    Map discipline: radial links show ecosystem roles and operating interfaces, not proof that every participant is involved in every placement or claim. Commercial edges require primary evidence naming the parties and relationship.

    Aircraft Hull & Machinery

    Physical loss or damage to aircraft, engines, components and equipment, subject to insured value, use, territory, deductibles and wording.

    • Airline and commercial fleets
    • Business and general aviation
    • Helicopters and rotorcraft
    • Aircraft spares and equipment

    Aviation Liability

    Liability from aircraft operation, passenger and cargo carriage, third-party injury/property damage and aviation service activities.

    • Passenger, baggage and cargo liability
    • Third-party and war liability
    • Airport owners and operators
    • Airside contractors and ground handlers

    Hull War & Allied Perils

    Separate clauses may address war, terrorism, hijacking, sabotage and related perils. Availability and scope depend on market conditions and wording.

    General Aviation

    Cover for business jets, private aircraft, helicopters, flight schools, charter operators and other non-airline aviation activity.

    Airport & Service-provider Liability

    Potential covers for airport operators, air traffic control, hangar keepers, MROs, refuellers, ground handlers and airside contractors.

    Specialist & Emerging Risks

    UAE insurer materials identify loss of licence, drones/UAS, satellite, manufacturers' liability, spares and aviation product risks. Each requires separate eligibility and wording review.

    GCAA minimum insurance requirements

    UAE AIP GEN 1.6 current publication; subject to stated exceptions and latest Montreal Convention limits.

    ExposureMinimum coverScope
    Passenger liability250,000 SDR per passengerNon-commercial aircraft MTOM ≤2,700 kg may be accepted at a lower minimum, not below 128,821 SDR.
    Baggage liability1,288 SDR per passengerCommercial operations.
    Cargo liability22 SDR per kgCommercial operations.
    Third-party liability750,000–700,000,000 SDR per accidentTiered by aircraft maximum take-off mass (MTOM).

    The GCAA text states that the latest Montreal Convention limits prevail where they differ. This is a concise display of the AIP table, not legal advice; verify the live AIP and applicable treaty limits before relying on it.

    Aviation insurance lifecycle

    Illustrative sequence; actual responsibilities depend on policy wording, contracts and regulation.

    01

    Risk submission

    Aircraft schedule, registration, values, fleet, routes, use, crew, operations, maintenance and loss history.

    02

    Underwriting & placement

    Insurer appetite, broker submissions, capacity, reinsurance support, limits, deductibles, war cover and exclusions.

    03

    Policy & compliance

    Policy schedule, endorsements, certificates, GCAA evidence and operator compliance checks.

    04

    Incident & notification

    Safety response, regulator notices, insurer/broker notice, evidence preservation and mitigation.

    05

    Claims assessment

    Coverage, insured interest, cause, quantum, liability, conditions, expert reports and conventions.

    06

    Settlement & recovery

    Settlement authority, payment, salvage, subrogation, contribution, litigation and reinsurance recoveries.

    Claims are not decided by aircraft type or incident label alone. Policy wording, insured parties, operational facts, causation, liability regime, evidence and applicable limits require authorised human review.

    • CBUAE — insurer and insurance-intermediary licensing. Insurance register
    • Airlines, business aviation, charter, helicopter, cargo and private operators.
    • Airports, air navigation, ground handlers, fuel providers, hangars, MROs and contractors.
