Evidence to collect

Evidence first; interpretation second.

  • Policy wording, benefits, exclusions and surrender terms.
  • Application disclosures and underwriting records.
  • Beneficiary verification, claim evidence and complaints.

Questions to validate

Potential friction points are hypotheses until supported by records.

  • Incomplete disclosures or unclear exclusions.
  • Beneficiary and identity verification gaps.
  • Long-term servicing and lapse communication.

Official regulatory starting points

  • CBUAE Rulebook · Insurance business classification — use to check the formal class taxonomy.
  • CBUAE · Licensing — verify licensed status and permitted activity.
  • DFSA · Insurance supervision — for relevant DIFC-regulated activity.
  • ADGM FSRA — check the relevant rulebook and regulated-entity register.
A regulatory classification is not evidence of market size, product availability, premium volume, loss performance or commercial opportunity. Keep documented facts separate from analysis and assumptions.
