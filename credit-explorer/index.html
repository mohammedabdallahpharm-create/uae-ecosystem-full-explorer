    Map discipline: radial links describe typical ecosystem roles and interfaces. They do not prove that every party participates in every policy, transaction or claim. Validate each relationship against the actual contract and evidence.
    Scope distinction: trade credit insurance protects defined receivables or financing exposures against insured non-payment events. Surety / guarantee insurance addresses an insured principal's failure to meet a defined obligation to a beneficiary. They are related but not interchangeable; coverage always depends on wording, eligibility, exclusions and applicable law.
