Role & responsibilities

    Key relationships

    Scope note: illustrative ecosystem research, not legal advice, a quotation or confirmation that every listed benefit is included in every product. Verify the policy schedule, wording, exclusions, limits and current regulatory requirements.

    Customer & protection value

    A clearly explained accident benefit can provide financial support after a covered event, subject to the policy's definitions, limits, waiting conditions and exclusions.

    • Transparent benefit schedules and eligibility.
    • Consistent evidence requirements for customers and providers.
    • Traceable decisions, explanations and appeal routes.

    Product & distribution opportunity

    Potential product designs include individual and group covers, employer-sponsored schemes and add-on benefits. These are market patterns to validate, not a statement that all designs are available from every UAE insurer.

    • Segment by occupation, group profile and distribution channel.
    • Explain accidental death, disability and medical-expense benefits separately.
    • Design service levels around evidence complexity and customer needs.

    Claims operations opportunity

    Accident claims often require joining event facts, medical outcomes, identity, beneficiary details and policy terms. Structured intake and transparent case tracking can reduce avoidable rework.

    • A single checklist by benefit type and claim scenario.
    • Clear hand-offs between intake, medical review and decision teams.
    • Reason codes and audit trails for every outcome.

    AI value & opportunity — human governed

    AI can prepare and organize claim work; authorized people retain coverage interpretation, medical judgment, fraud conclusions and final decisions.

    • OCR and extraction from claim forms, medical reports and police reports.
    • Document completeness, date consistency and duplicate-document flags.
    • Policy-clause retrieval with source citations and version control.
    • Triage suggestions and customer status updates, with human review.
    • Fraud indicators treated as leads only; no automated adverse decision.

    Data, governance & controls

    • Role-based access and minimum-necessary personal data.
    • Secure handling of sensitive medical and identity documents.
    • Model testing for accuracy, bias, drift and explainability.
    • Human override, escalation and documented accountability.

    Evidence-led opportunity questions

    • Which benefit types create the highest document friction?
    • Where do cases wait: employer, provider, claimant or insurer?
    • Can a standard evidence pack improve first-time completeness?
    • Which decisions require specialist review by design?

    Product, disclosure & eligibility

    Confirm insured persons, benefit schedule, sum insured, occupation or group conditions, effective dates and exclusions.

    Accident notification

    Record date, time, location, event description, parties involved and the benefit being claimed.

    Evidence collection

    Request relevant medical records, incident or police report where applicable, identity, beneficiary and employer documents.

    Coverage & benefit assessment

    Match facts and medical outcome to definitions, limits, exclusions, waiting conditions and policy period.

    Human review & decision

    Escalate ambiguity, complex disability assessment, conflicting evidence and exceptions to authorized reviewers.

    Settlement & communication

    Apply the contractual benefit, document the rationale and communicate outcome, missing items or next steps.

    Review, complaint & appeal

    Provide the applicable internal review and complaint route, preserving records and response timelines.

    Portfolio learning

    Monitor turnaround, completeness, dispute patterns, benefit mix and service quality without exposing personal data.

    The flow is a reference operating model. Actual evidence, time limits, benefit calculations and escalation routes must be taken from the specific policy and applicable UAE requirements.

    The issued contract, schedule, endorsements and applicable exclusions are the primary evidence for covered persons, insured events, benefits, limits and conditions in an individual claim.

    Case-level source · Obtain the exact version applicable on the date of loss.
