Evidence rule

Legal classification confirms a recognised line, not market size, demand, loss experience or commercial opportunity.

How to read this prototype

  • Verified fact means the linked source directly supports the factual statement; it does not verify the interpretation.
  • Regulatory baseline means an official rule or framework is relevant, but it is not presented as a new market trend.
  • Market validation questions are intentionally open. This MVP does not claim that demand, pain, or ROI has been proven.
  • Scores, forecasts, unsupported market-share claims and automatic decisions are excluded from this first version.
