What the figures say

OFFICIAL FACT

Premium growth

CBUAE's 2025 Annual Report records gross written premiums rising from AED 65.1bn in 2024 to AED 75.2bn in 2025 (+15.5% year on year).

Read CBUAE Annual Report 2025 ↗

OFFICIAL FACT

Claims activity

Gross paid claims increased from AED 41.6bn in 2024 to AED 46.2bn in 2025 (+11.0%), according to the same report.

Source: CBUAE, Table 8 ↗

Licensed market

CBUAE reports 58 licensed insurance companies in 2025 and 516 related insurance professions. The live 2026 entity list is maintained by CBUAE and should be used for current entity-level checks.

ANALYSIS · NOT A REGULATORY FACT

Opportunity: claims operations

A growing premium base and higher paid claims make claims quality, documentation completeness, leakage controls and evidence-backed human review useful areas to investigate. This is a reasoned opportunity hypothesis—not proof of a specific employer's demand.

OFFICIAL REGULATORY SOURCE

DHA professional conduct standards (2025)

The published standards require accurate billing codes and truthful, timely, complete documentation supporting insurance claims and audits, and prohibit duplicate claims and upcoding. These provisions support the explorer's claims-integrity and documentation themes; they are not a substitute for payer-specific adjudication rules.

Read DHA standards PDF ↗

Scope note: DHA source is specific to Dubai and the stated professional standards. Do not generalize it to every emirate or every insurer rule.

Data reconciliation note

CBUAE publications are not fully aligned on the 2025 gross written premium total: its 2025 Annual Report reports AED 75.2bn, while the separate Annual Report on the UAE Insurance Sector for 2025 has been reported as AED 74.8bn. This page deliberately attributes AED 75.2bn to the Annual Report cited above rather than treating the two figures as interchangeable. For cross-publication comparison, confirm definitions and tables before reuse.
