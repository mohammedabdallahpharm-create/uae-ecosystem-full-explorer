First comparative finding

The key distinction is not simply “UAE vs Saudi.” Saudi health-insurance mandates are under a national Insurance Authority, while the UAE combines federal insurance rules with emirate-specific health-insurance requirements. For claims operations, the practical questions are where the licence sits, which local rules apply, and which current claims/data standards govern the workflow—not whether the markets are identical.
