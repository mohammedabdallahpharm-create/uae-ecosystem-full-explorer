Understand an insurance ecosystem in 5 minutes

You do not need to learn everything at once. Use this short path:

  1. Start with the centre: identify the insured person, member or organisation.
  2. Follow the participants: see who pays, provides service, distributes cover or regulates.
  3. Trace the journey: follow the flow from need or policy to service, claim and outcome.
  4. Open one detail: select a node or sector and read its role before exploring more.
  5. Check the evidence: distinguish official facts from analytical interpretation and items needing verification.

Tip: focus on one question at a time. This is an orientation map, not a substitute for policy wording or official regulation.
